# How to edit your website

Your website updates itself whenever you ask Claude for a change. You don't need any technical knowledge.

## Every time

1. Open **claude.ai/code**, or click **Code** in the Claude app.
2. Start a **New session** and choose your website's repository.
3. Say what you want, in plain words. For example:
   - "Change the price of my one-to-one sessions to £90."
   - "Add this testimonial from Sarah: …"
   - "Replace the top photo with the one I've attached."
   - "Add my retreat on 14 March in Glastonbury, £350, with this booking link: …"
   - "Make the headline say: …"
4. Claude makes the change and publishes it. It's live in about a minute.
5. Check it on your phone. If you see the old version, close the tab and reopen it.

## Good to know

| Want to… | Say |
|---|---|
| Undo the last change | "Undo that last change." |
| See before it's live | "Show me the change before you publish it." |
| Keep your exact words | "Use my wording exactly." |
| Change only one thing | "Change only the headline, nothing else." |

## Never put on the website
- Bank details or card numbers
- Clients' names, stories or health details without written permission
- Your home address
