# Client intake: send before the setup call

Ask the client to answer in a document or an email, or as a voice note. Rough answers are fine. Claude turns them into website copy.

## About you
1. Your name, business name, and how you want to be described in one line (e.g. "Psychotherapist in Bristol").
2. Who do you help? Describe your ideal client in a few sentences.
3. What are your clients struggling with when they find you? Give three examples, in their words if you can.
4. What changes for them after working with you?
5. How do you work: your method, training and approach?
6. Your story: how did you come to this work? (A few paragraphs.)
7. Qualifications, memberships and registrations (e.g. BACP, UKCP, insurance).
8. Where do you work: town, in person, online?

## Offers
9. List each offer: name, what is included, length, price.
10. Should prices be shown publicly, or only on a private page you send after a call?
11. Payment and cancellation policy.

## Proof
12. Up to three testimonials, word for word, with permission and the name format to use (full name, initials, or "client").
13. Any results, numbers, press or notable work.

## Links and tools
14. Booking link (Calendly, Acuity, Cal.com, etc.).
15. Stripe payment links, one per offer, if taking payment online.
16. Social links: Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.
17. Do you have a mailing list? Which tool (Brevo, Mailchimp, none)?
18. Do you already have a domain? Where was it bought (Wix, GoDaddy, Squarespace…)?
19. Your business email address.

## Look and feel
20. Three to five photos of you: one for the top of the page, one relaxed portrait, one more. Good light and a plain background work best.
21. Colours or brands you love, and anything you dislike.
22. Three words for how your site should feel (e.g. calm, warm, grounded).

## Legal (for the privacy policy)
23. Your legal trading name (sole trader name or company name and number).
24. Business address for the policy (a registered or virtual address is fine; it can't be blank).
25. ICO registration number, if you have one.
